PrintCal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PrintCal works entirely on your device. The photos, birthdays and events you add are stored locally on your PC and are never uploaded to any server.

Printed pages, pictures and backup files are created on your PC and saved only where you choose.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
