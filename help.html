PrintCal – How to use

Make your own wall calendar with family photos, birthdays and events, and print it at home.

1. Make your first calendar

  1. Open PrintCal. A calendar for the coming year is ready on the right.
  2. On the left, check the Year and choose your Paper: Letter (US) or A4.
  3. Click a month button above the preview (Jan, Feb, …) and click Add a photo to give that month a picture.
  4. Add birthdays and events (section 3).
  5. Click 🖨 Print all 12 months.

Want to see a finished example first? Click ☰ More → Load the sample calendar.

2. Choose the look

All settings are in the left panel:

  • Page style – Photo on top, month below for a classic wall calendar, or Big month grid, no photo with large boxes to write in.
  • Week starts on – Sunday or Monday.
  • Language – month and day names in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish or Vietnamese.
  • Lettering – Modern or Classic letters.
  • Color – click one of the colored dots.
  • Show US holidays – prints the US public holidays on their dates. Untick it if you do not need them.

3. Add birthdays and events

  1. Under Birthdays & events choose the month and type the day.
  2. Choose the kind: 🎂 Birthday, ♥ Anniversary or • Other.
  3. Type the name or event, for example Grandma Rose.
  4. Click Add. The entry appears in the list and on the calendar page.

To remove an entry, click the ✕ next to it in the list.

Birthdays and anniversaries come back every year – change the Year next December and your dates are already there.

4. Add photos

  1. Click the month you want.
  2. Click Add a photo and choose a picture from your PC. Wide (landscape) photos fill the space best.
  3. To change it, click the button again and pick another picture. Remove takes the photo off that month.

A small dot on a month button shows that the month already has a photo. Months without a photo get a simple drawn picture.

5. Print

  • 🖨 Print all 12 months – twelve pages, one for each month.
  • Print this month – only the month you are looking at.
  • ☰ More → Print the whole year on one page (Pro) – a one-page year planner.

In the Windows print window choose the same paper size as in PrintCal, and turn off “Headers and footers” if you see that option. Thicker paper (120–160 g) makes a nicer calendar.

6. Save a month as a picture

Click 🖼 Save picture to save the month you are looking at as a PNG picture in your Downloads folder – handy for sending to family or to a print shop.

7. Bring in birthdays from Kinleaf

If you use our family tree app Kinleaf: in Kinleaf save a backup file, then in PrintCal click ☰ More → Add birthdays from a Kinleaf family tree backup… and choose that file. Everyone with a full date of birth is added as a birthday.

8. Keep your calendar safe: backups

Your calendar is stored on this PC only. Click ☰ More → Save a backup file to save everything, photos included, into one file. Open a backup file… brings it back, also on another PC. Start a new empty calendar… clears everything.

9. Free version and Pro

The free version includes all styles, languages, printing and backups, with up to 3 photos and 12 birthdays or events. Pro is a one-time purchase and adds a photo for every month, unlimited birthdays and events, and the whole year on one page. Click ★ Get Pro at the top to see the price, or Restore purchase if you bought it before.

10. If something goes wrong

  • The page is cut off or too small on paper: check that the paper size in the print window matches PrintCal, and set Scale to 100% or “Fit to page”.
  • A photo looks blurry: use the original photo file, not a small copy from a chat app.
  • The day is not accepted: type a day that exists in that month, for example 1–30 for April.
